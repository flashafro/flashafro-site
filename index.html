About

Hi, I'm Cheryl.

Senior creative leader with over fifteen years building brand systems at scale across Prime Video, HBO Max, Apple TV+, Paramount+, Crunchyroll and more, across ten markets.

FlashAfro.com is my R&D lab. A place to experiment openly, document what I'm learning, and make things because I'm curious about what's possible.

Some people might be asking where the 'FlashAfro' name came from. Well... it started with Flash cartoons. FlashAfro was my online pen name back in the day. Back when I use to make little flash cartoons (RIP Flash 😭) mostly for myself but also for the fun flash animation community that I found myself joining. There were no briefs, no clients, just me playing with some cool tools and seeing what could happen. That's still how I work today. Play is where the good stuff begins. Learning through play has always been the way that I broaden my creative skillset and these AI tools feel exactly like that to me. A new playground for my fully eccentric self.
